# Changelog

All notable changes to `LeafVideo` (`:leaf-video` & `LCV-ICD-1.0`) are documented in this file.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-08

### Added
- **Normative Binary Interface Control Document (`LCV-ICD-1.0`, `leaf-video/docs/ICD.md`, MIT):** Defines the self-indexing `64-byte` `"LCV_V001"` `Superblock` header (with reflected IEEE 802.3 `CRC-32` over bytes `0x00..0x3B`), 2D and `16/32/64` quadtree-coalesced `uint64` tile pointer directories (`LCV_COLOR_FLAG_QUADTREE_DIR = 0x80`), Extended Hamming `(72, 64)` `SEC-DED` pointer syndrome table (`1 B/word`), split Section 4A (Base MSB Slice Pool) and Section 4B (Tail LSB Slice Pool with direct $O(1)$ `lsbTileOffsets[N_tiles]`), non-temporal log-structured slack/superseded framing (`LCV_FLAG_SUPERSEDED_OR_SLACK = 0x10`), Matroska `.mkv` (`CodecID = "V_LCV1"`) and `LCVU` UDP datagram framing, and `tuningProfile16` operating profiles (`ARCHIVAL_LOSSLESS`, `OBS_REALTIME_LOSSLESS`, `WIRELESS_FOVEATED_LOW_LATENCY`, `HARSH_RF_UEP_ZERO_WAIT`, `VR_STEREO_FOVEATED_DIRECT`, `STUDIO_MEZZANINE_12BIT`).
- **Canonical Native C11 / SIMD / GPU Codec & Compressed-Domain Transformer (`resource/LeafVideo/native/`, `leaf-video-ADR-001` & `leaf-video-ADR-004`):**
  - `leafvideo.h` & `lcv_accel.c`: 3D (`H_3`) and column-Hilbert (`T+H_2`) LUTs, 1-pass directional anisotropy curve selection, `(72, 64)` `SEC-DED` pointer healing (`lcv_heal_single_bit_flip64`), reversible $\mathbb{Z}_{2^B}$ `RGBA <-> YCoCg-R` lifting across $B \in \{8, 10, 12\}$, `deltaFlags8` tagged sub-modes (`HILBERT_1D_MSB`, `INTRA_CUBE_TEMPORAL`, `SPATIAL_MED_2D` with `CCLM-MSB` `cclmSlopes8` and $4 \times 4$ sub-tile bilinear motion shear, and `PLANAR_3D_MV` + `WAVELET_3D_HAAR` with `compactHdr` and `maxU8` bitplane conditioning), 2-pass integer bounded-interval POCS stripped-LSB restoration (`lcv_restore_stripped_field_bounded_interval_tyx`), neighbor-MV optical-flow and cold-anchor quarantine concealment, size-prefixed Zstd pool compaction (`lcv_native_compact_superblock_zstd`), and zero-CPU-callback CUDA PTX / Vulkan compute (`lcv_cube_kernel.comp`) encoding.
  - `lcv_codec_system_plugins.c`, `obs_leafvideo_plugin.c` & `lcv_cli_and_test.c`: Container probing (`lcv_probe_stream_file`), single-file Section 4A 8-bit preview scrubbing (`lcv_native_decode_superblock_rgba8_preview`), `libobs v30` studio integration, and the native C11 Gate 1 verification suite (`./gradlew :leaf-video:test`).
- **Three-Tier Open Standard & Commercial Product Split (`leaf-video-ADR-002` & `resource/LeafVideo/`):** Establishes Tier 1 (`LCV-ICD-1.0` & `:leaf-video` under MIT), Tier 2 Creator & Editor Binaries (OBS Studio "Single-Encode Multi-Crop & Crash Guard" Suite and NLE Instant-Scrub & Crash-Resumable Render Plugin), and Tier 3 High-Performance Native SIMD/GPU & Wireless Foveated UEP SDK (`LicenseRef-LeafCraft-Commercial`), projected to `https://github.com/LeafCraftDev/LeafVideo`.
